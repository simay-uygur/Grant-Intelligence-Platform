NORTHLIGHT ROBOTICS GMBH - COMPANY PROFILE
===========================================

Organisation Overview
---------------------
Northlight Robotics GmbH is a German SME founded in 2015 and headquartered in
Munich, Bavaria. The company designs, builds, and operates autonomous inspection
robotics for industrial infrastructure, with a current team of 25 employees
(14 engineers, 6 robotics researchers, 5 operations and sales staff).

Track Record
------------
- 2024: Delivered 40 autonomous drone inspection pilots across 12 EU ports,
  including Rotterdam, Hamburg, Antwerp, and Valencia.
- 2023: Certified under ISO 9001:2015; achieved TRL 7 on the core
  CorrosionScan visual-inspection platform.
- 2022: Winner of the Bavarian Innovation Award for applied robotics.

Financials
----------
- 2025 revenue: EUR 2.1 million (60% B2B contracts, 40% pilot projects)
- 2024 revenue: EUR 1.4 million
- Profitable since 2024; reinvesting 30% of profit into R&D.

Key Capabilities
----------------
1. Autonomous navigation in GPS-denied environments (proprietary SLAM stack).
2. AI-based corrosion and crack detection (94% precision on validated datasets).
3. Edge deployment on embedded hardware; no cloud dependency for inspection runs.
4. In-house certification workflow aligned with EU machinery regulations.
