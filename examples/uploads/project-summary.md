# Project Summary: ScanGuard AI

## Problem
Europe's 300+ commercial ports lose an estimated EUR 800 million per year to
undetected steel corrosion on cranes, hulls, and quay walls. Manual inspection
is slow, expensive (EUR 4k per crane cycle), and dangerous — rope-access teams
account for 12 reported serious injuries in EU ports annually.

## Proposed Solution
ScanGuard AI extends our proven CorrosionScan platform with fully autonomous
docking stations: drones permanently stationed inside port terminals perform
nightly inspection sweeps without human operators, using edge AI to detect and
classify corrosion at TRL 8 fidelity.

## Innovation
- Self-docking + self-charging drone nests rated for marine environments (IP67)
- On-board defect classification in under 900 ms (no connectivity needed)
- Predictive maintenance API feeding directly into port asset-management systems

## Objectives (36-month project)
1. TRL 8 validation across 6 climatically diverse EU ports
2. Reduce per-crane inspection cost by 70% vs manual baseline
3. Achieve class-leading 96% defect-detection recall
4. Prepare CE conformity and enter commercial scale-up in year 3

## Consortium Needs
We seek one university partner (computer vision / edge AI) and one port
authority as a pilot site. Budget envelope sought: EUR 1.8–2.5 million total.
